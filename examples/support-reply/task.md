# Task: customer support reply

The rubric in this example grades replies written by a support agent (human or AI) to this ticket.

## Customer message

> Hi, I was charged twice for my March subscription ($14.99 on March 3, two times). Can I get the duplicate refunded, and how long will it take? — Sam

## Policy context given to the agent and the grader

- Duplicate charges are refunded on request. The agent can issue the refund directly; the customer does not need to fill in a form.
- Refunds reach the customer's card in **5 to 7 business days**, depending on the bank.
- Replies are plain-text emails sent straight to the customer.
